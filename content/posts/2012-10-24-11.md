["From $0 To $1 Billion In Two Quarters – Facebook's Mobile Ad Business Is Suddenly Huge"](http://www.businessinsider.com/starting-from-0-facebook-has-created-a-1-billion-mobile-ad-business-in-just-two-quarters-2012-10)> 75% of $4 million is $3 million. $3 million per day over 365 days is…$1.1 billion.

 Good explanation for part of the reason why I’m confident in Facebook’s long term revenue growth. For example, mobile revenue has grown from $1 million per day at the end of Q2 to $3 million per day at the end of Q3.
