## Long Story Short... My recommendations for Books, Movies, TV Shows, Websites, Bands, and More...

## Non-Fiction Book Recommendation:

### [Switch: How To Change Things When Change Is Hard](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0385528752)

Interesting stories and anecdotes that teach you the science behind "Change". ## Movie Recommendation:

### [Submarine](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B005C7SXMS)

Underrated coming of age story. It's easy well made, has a unique style, and many moments that ring true. ## TV Show Recommendation:

### [The Profit](http://www.cnbc.com/live-tv/the-profit)

Reality TV at its best. An entrepreneur invests money in failing small businesses and tries to turn them around. Very interesting stuff. Season 3 begins Tuesday, May 12th 2015 at 10pm ET/PT on CNBC. Old episodes can be found on CNBC or [HERE](http://www.cnbc.com/live-tv/the-profit). ## Website Recommendation:

### [Twitter.com](https://twitter.com/andrewstepner/)

The world's Most Misunderstood, Most Poorly Explained, yet Most Amazing website. Twitter is secretly all about reading stuff, not posting stuff. Customizable to your interests, fast, behind-the-scenes, and links that matter. ## Band Recommendation:

### [Bronze Radio Return](http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00CS9NIO6)

Little known band. Great music.
